The kick-off plan September 2026, the story every campaign serves

What we sell

A curated, bilingual, ad-free super app for family life. Nobody paid to be recommended. No agency commissions. Members pay for independence, and independence is a feeling, not a feature. Insider: first week 0.49, then 1 euro a week, no free trial, cancel in one tap. Founding Family badge for the first 90 days.

The funnel

Ad asks a question about the family, never about the app. Web quiz, 5 to 7 questions. Result card, half visible, shareable as a story. Insider welcome with the price on screen. Then the hooks: Dailies, Favorites, Playdates, Babysitters.

Channels

Attention on Meta (one ad set per angle, four creatives, review weekly, kill losers, scale winners 20 to 30 percent). Intent on Apple Ads and Google (kinderfeestje Amsterdam, kindvriendelijk restaurant Amsterdam, basisschool Amsterdam kiezen, babysitter Amsterdam, wat te doen met kinderen Amsterdam). Trust on Instagram organic: the 8 formats.

The bar to beat

60% quiz completion. 5% quiz completers to Insider (10 is the dream). 45% first renewal. Search subscribers 3x cheaper than Meta. Read the numbers against this every Monday in the quiz funnel on the Dashboard.

Eight weeks

W1 pricing FAQ, paywall, 0.49 intro, rename Food AI to Dinner Sorted. W2 web quiz at /quiz, result cards. W3 film Fridge Rescue, launch Meta and Apple Ads, babysitter supply. W4 to 5 hosted playdates filmed, Founding Family badge live, first member gift newsletter. W6 to 8 scale the winning angle, second quiz, partner badge wave.

Creative rules

Phone shot beats polished. The hook is a question. One moment per ad. Price on screen, honestly. Result cards are ads: 1080x1920 in our palette, members post it, we repost it. Test, kill, scale.

Campaigns status and notes are per city. Organic from the Ibiza playbook, paid and retention from the kick-off plan
